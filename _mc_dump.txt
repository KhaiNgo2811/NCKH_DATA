1. Purpose and authority
This codebook is the variable-level bridge between three artefacts that must never disagree: the Qualtrics instrument (PROJ_MAIN_final_v2), the analysis pipeline (src/_config.py and scripts 00–07), and the governing specification (Theoretical Foundations v2.12). Where any two disagree, TF v2.12 prevails on substance and this codebook records the reconciliation.
Every entry below matches PROJ_MAIN_final_v2, the wording finalized for main collection under Amendment A-13 — not the earlier pilot-stage wording. Pilot (n = 46) reliability statistics quoted in this codebook were computed on the pre-A-13 wording for AIP, INT, TRU, PDPL, and PI; they are indicative of construct behaviour, not confirmatory of this exact item text. See §2, v2.2.
Verification basis (Amendment A-20). Every item stem in §4.7 below was checked character-by-character against the question-text header row of the live Qualtrics export (data/raw/pilot_real.csv, 481 responses, 15 September 2026). All 34 stems match B1.5 — Measurement Scales v3 exactly. Where this codebook previously disagreed with B1.5 — the TRU item set and wording, ENG wording, PI1–PI2 wording, INT1–INT5 English wording, REL4 provenance, and the PDPL statutory anchors — B1.5 is correct and this codebook was stale. Those entries are corrected here; the instrument is not changed. PDPL1–PDPL4 item wording and administration are unaffected by Amendments A-16/A-17 — both concern only where the statute’s name is documented (never in the fielded instrument, per A-17); see §4.7a.
Reading rule. “Fielded” means the respondent was asked the item and it exists in the raw export. “Analysed” means the item enters the measurement model. These are not the same set — see §5.2.

REL — Perceived Relevance (4 items, all analysed)
Block M_REL. RETRACTED SOURCE: the previous attribution to Xu et al. (2011) and the intermediate attribution to Shanahan, Tran & Taylor (2019) "via Aydin (2026)" were both checked against primary-source PDFs; neither contains matching items. Both citation chains are retracted, not superseded — no item in REL should ever be re-attributed to Aydin (2026) in any of its versions. Current sourcing (verified item-level, PDF-checked): REL1 adapted from Srinivasan, Anderson & Ponnavolu (2002), Journal of Retailing, 78(1), item (a). REL2 and REL3 adapted from Yin, Qiu & Wang (2025), JTAER 20(1), Art. 21, "Relevance Experience (RE)" dimension, items RE4 and RE1 respectively (REL2 = category fit, "type of product I was looking for"; REL3 = search-history fit, "what I had previously searched for"; same source paper as AIP, different and discriminantly-validated dimension — r = .622 in the source paper — so no item-level overlap with AIP). REL4 provenance — single ratified chain (Amendment A-20). REL4 (“Overall, the product list I saw was useful to me”) derives from a 2-item semantic-differential pair (“relevant/irrelevant”; “not at all useful / of very great use”) originating in an information-diagnosticity scale, Klar (1990), as reproduced in Ahluwalia, Unnava & Burnkrant (2001), Journal of Marketing Research, 38(4) — where the 3-item diagnosticity scale reports α = .70 and carries a third “indicative / not indicative” anchor, dropped here as too specific to diagnosticity. The pair was repurposed as a measure of perceived relevance by De Keyzer, Dens & De Pelsmacker (2015), Journal of Interactive Advertising, 15(2), who report α = .937 for that repurposed use — a figure describing De Keyzer et al.’s own data, not Ahluwalia et al.’s. This is the only ratified provenance for REL4. Any attribution of REL4 to Yin, Qiu & Wang (2025) is withdrawn as an error. That attribution appeared in TF §A.2, the TF A-9 amendment row, and this codebook; all three are corrected by Amendment A-20. Yin’s Relevance Experience dimension supplies REL2 and REL3 only, and contains no item matching REL4’s wording. Carried open flag (OD-16): Klar (1990) has not been independently verified at item level — REL4 is the only item in the 34-item battery without a PDF-verified primary source, which is in tension with the project’s citation-integrity rule. Resolve by further sourcing or self-development before final ratification. Separately, Amendment A-9’s REL4-exclusion logic no longer applies: the item formerly coded REL4 (Tam & Ho, 2006, decision-usefulness wording) was removed from the instrument entirely in an earlier pass, and the current REL4 is an unrelated item — do not confuse the two.

Wording discipline. REL items reference the appraised outcome only (matches my needs, consistent with my situation/search history, useful to me) and deliberately avoid any mechanism verb ("tailor," "customize," "adjust," "personalize," "analyze") reserved for AIP. REL2's source item originally used "tailored to my situation" (Srinivasan item c); this was replaced with the current RE4-based wording specifically to remove that action-verb overlap with AIP's register.
Residual risk. REL4 carries an open governance flag (repurposed generic item pair, primary source Klar 1990 not independently verified) and should be prioritised for either further sourcing or self-development before formal ratification. Pilot statistics from earlier item wording (α = .947, historical) do not apply to the current 4-item set; REL requires a new pilot pass or expert content-validity review before main collection.

Rationale, recorded so the reversal stays auditable. (i) Consistency with the structural model. ENG_mean — one pooled composite — has always been the sole structural input for H5, H6b, E1 and E2. 8. Standing prohibitions
These are the errors that have actually occurred or been narrowly avoided on this project. Each is cheap to prevent and expensive to discover late.
No DISC measurement model. No α, loadings, AVE, CR or HTMT for DISC, ever. No DISC1/DISC2 column may be created.
No three-way interaction. β_M3 and β_M4 do not exist. Retired by A-8.
RETIRED RULE — no longer applies. This rule (“REL4 excluded from the measurement model”) applied only to the earlier REL4 item (Tam & Ho, 2006 wording), which has since been removed from the instrument entirely. The current REL4 (Yin, Qiu & Wang, 2025) is a different item and is fully included in α, AVE, HTMT, loadings and REL_mean, alongside REL1–REL3. Do not exclude it.
RETIRED RULE — no longer applies (Amendment A-20). The prohibition “no pooled reliability across all six ENG items” (A-12) is withdrawn. ENG α, item-rest correlation, outer loadings, CR, AVE and EFA/CFA are computed on the pooled six-item set, like every other construct; the three-dimension labels survive as content description only. ENG must appear in every reliability, loadings and CR/AVE table — its absence from those tables is now a defect, not compliance.
No dropping ENG4. Unchanged by A-20. Dropping ENG4 leaves Activation represented by ENG5 alone and deletes the dimension Hollebeek et al. treat as behaviourally diagnostic. A weak pooled loading is reported in Limitations, not resolved by item deletion. Any removal requires a dedicated amendment.
No “PDPD” and no “Decree 13/2023/NĐ-CP” as the operative instrument, in any code, comment, variable name, output label, figure or manuscript text. The anchor is Law 91/2025/QH15.
No second test of H3. The ANOVA main effect is the confirmatory test; the SEM’s DISC → INT path is descriptive.
No Study 2 metrics in Study 1. Precision@10, NDCG@10 and Green Exposure Rate belong to the DSR artifact evaluation and never appear in Study 1 reporting. Study 2 carries no H-numbered hypothesis.
No ASCII-stripped Vietnamese anchors. Consent and screener anchors must retain full diacritics; an ASCII anchor matches zero real responses and silently drops the whole sample.
No confirmatory PLS-SEM on pilot data. The pilot is reliability, EFA and manipulation checks only.
No −98 in a computed statistic. Recode “prefer not to say” to missing before any demographic summary.
No silent item-count drift. Any change to the item set is an amendment to TF first, then this codebook, then _config.py — in that order.
No two sources of truth for item wording. B1.5 v3 is the item-level source of truth for stems, source attribution and response format; this codebook is the variable-level source of truth for coding, derivation and exclusion. Where they overlap they must be identical — and as of Amendment A-20 they are, both having been verified against the live export.
No sign-unnormalised loadings in a reported table. A single-factor extraction is sign-indeterminate, so an entire construct can emerge with uniformly negative loadings (observed at n = 277 for AIP, REL, TRU, PI and PDPL). Orient each construct’s loading vector positive before reporting; a negative loading in a manuscript table reads as a coding error even when its magnitude is correct.
No personal data in a committed file, ever. IPAddress, LocationLatitude, LocationLongitude, recipient name/email fields and ExternalReference are personal data under Law 91/2025/QH15 Art. 2. They must be stripped from raw and processed exports before any commit, and must never sit in a publicly readable repository. See §9, OD-15.
